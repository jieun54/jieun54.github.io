I am a Ph.D. candidate at the [University of Michigan School of Information](https://www.si.umich.edu/), working with [Libby Hemphill](https://www.libbyh.com/). As a computational social scientist, I study how human behavior and social interaction in online spaces emerge through the interplay of users, social contexts, and technologies. Specifically, my doctoral research focuses on how technological interventions, such as platform-specific features and general-purpose technologies, interact with existing social dynamics to produce different outcomes across user groups in online communities. 

Methodologically, I use data science techniques to collect and analyze large-scale digital trace data from diverse online communities. I also completed data science internships at [Roblox](https://corp.roblox.com/) and [Meta](https://www.meta.com/about/company-info/). I generate social scientific insights about how users interact with both people and AI in online spaces and translate those insights into evidence-based design recommendations for platforms and practitioners.
